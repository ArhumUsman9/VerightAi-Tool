# Victim-Safe Response Skill

## Purpose
Provides trauma-informed, privacy-centric forensic verification and rapid response guidance for survivors of non-consensual synthetic imagery (NCII), sextortion, and malicious digital impersonation.

## Privacy & Safety Mandates
1. **Zero Persistent Storage**: Sensitive uploaded evidence must never be saved to persistent databases or disks without explicit consent.
2. **Third-Party Boundary**: Do not transmit full-resolution sensitive or intimate imagery to third-party generative APIs.
3. **Redaction in Public Reports**: Strip identifying personal markers, phone numbers, and sensitive GPS coordinates from exported reports.
4. **Evidence Preservation**:
   - Compute and log SHA-256 cryptographic hashes immediately for legal admissibility under FRE 901 / Daubert criteria.
   - Maintain original container metadata intact without modification.
   - Document URL sources, timestamps, and platform distribution identifiers.
5. **Support Channels**:
   - Provide direct links to StopNCII.org (hashes for cross-platform prevention) and TakeItDown.ncmec.org (for youth/minors).
   - Offer bilingual reporting guidance in English and Urdu (FIA Cybercrime Helpline 1991, Digital Rights Foundation Helpline).
